Security & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026 • Rivava Financial Technologies

1. Executive Overview

At Rivava, we treat financial privacy as a fundamental human right. We are dedicated to providing research, learning, and tracking tools with transparent data handling practices. We do not sell your personal data or financial logs to advertising brokers or credit agencies.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Identification: Name, verified email address, and phone number for WhatsApp advisory routing.
  • User-Provided Data: Voluntary financial allocations, watchlists, savings goals, and transaction categories submitted through our tracking calculators or challenge forms.
  • Technical Metadata: Anonymized telemetry to optimize application performance, prevent malicious scraping of research reports, and maintain uptime.

3. Cryptographic Data Security

All sensitive information stored within Rivava's infrastructure is protected via industry-standard AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit. Financial APIs utilized for tracking operate under strict read-only protocols.

4. Advisory Communications

When you initiate conversation with our advisors via WhatsApp or phone, communications are conducted directly through verified business channels. You may request deletion of past advisory conversation logs at any time by emailing support@rivava.com.

5. Regulatory & Financial Disclaimer

Rivava provides educational content and market research models. We are not a portfolio management service (PMS) or broker. Past performance of any company covered does not guarantee future results.
