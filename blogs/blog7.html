Fund Research Basics: How Fee Drag Destroys Compounding Returns.

Adarsh Chandra, CAO
Dec 09, 2024 6 Min Read

Most investors understand the magic of compound interest. Very few understand the inverse: the silent tyranny of compounding management fees. A seemingly small 1.5% annual expense ratio difference can confiscate over 35% of your final accumulated net worth over a 30-year investing horizon.

1. The Math of Fee Drag

Assume an investment of ₹10,000 per month earning 12% gross annual returns over 25 years.

  • Low-Cost Direct Index Fund (0.1% TER): Final value ~ ₹1.84 Crores.
  • Actively Managed Regular Fund (1.8% TER): Final value ~ ₹1.31 Crores.

That single 1.7% fee delta cost you over ₹53 Lakhs in lost wealth that compounded in the asset manager's pockets rather than yours.

2. Evaluating Tracking Error

When selecting a passive index fund (e.g. Nifty 50 or S&P 500), the goal is not to beat the market; it is to replicate the index with minimal deviation. Tracking error measures how closely the fund manager matches the underlying benchmark.

Always look for funds with lower tracking error (< 0.05%) and large AUM to avoid liquidity slip and cash drag during dividend distributions.
